Projects

Things I've worked on, in no particular order:

ipcheq2

Investigate IPs with data from AbuseIPDB, VirusTotal, your own local VPNId, and the iCloud private relay list, all in one space. Self-host your own API for first-party VPN identification (optionally) without north-south traffic! Check out the writeup here and the repo on GitHub.

Version 2.3 out now!

LogCrunch

A prototype Log Aggregator for cyberdefense competitions and small networks, as well as my Honors Undergrad Thesis at Oregon State. Optimized for minimal resource consumption and near-instant setup. Under active developments, with the paper coming soon. Check our the source code here.

USBeaver

A project for DAMSEC to teach about hotplug hacking and rouge HID devices. Check out the writeup and slides here!
